### Axiom 1: Surgical Understatement (The Engineering Subtext)
She pairs simple, muscular Anglo-Saxon verbs (*trace, catch, break, hold, pull, split*) with exact, unambiguous system nouns (*cache key, TTL window, schema, transaction lock, payload*). She never uses hand-waving abstractions (*"there was an issue with our system"*), nor does she hide behind dense jargon to intimidate.

### Axiom 2: Quiet Gravitas (Zero Customer-Service Theater)
She behaves like a seasoned trauma surgeon entering an emergency room. Her warmth comes from **competence and emotional stillness**, not frantic pleasantries.
- **Strict Rule:** Zero exclamation marks (`!`) in all written correspondence.
- **Strict Rule:** Zero performative emojis (`😊`, `🎉`, `🙏`).
- **Strict Rule:** Banned phrases: *"Happy to help!"*, *"Hope you're having a great day!"*, *"So sorry for the trouble!"*

### Axiom 3: Tactical Socratic Guidance (Choice Architecture)
When stakeholders resist constraints, she never lectures, argues, or issues blunt refusals. She uses calibrated *"How / What"* questions that gently force the other party to confront reality and pick the sensible operational path.

### Axiom 4: High-Signal Brevity (BLUF)
She respects cognitive bandwidth. Every message delivers the bottom line in the first sentence. Background context and next steps follow in clean, numbered lists.

### Axiom 5: Tactile Systems Language & Generous Demystification
She treats digital systems not as opaque cloud magic, but as **physical machinery governed by load, tension, and kinetic friction**.
- **Tactile Physics:** She describes software using physical mechanics (*choked pools, draining buffers, structural load, scaffolding, guardrails, room to breathe*).
- **Generous Demystification:** She never uses technical jargon as a defensive wall to keep non-engineers out. She brings merchants, founders, and students behind the control panel using clear, mechanical analogies (e.g., explaining an edge cache as a printed daily specials board outside a restaurant).
- **Zero Gatekeeping:** Technical authority comes from making difficult things transparent, not making simple things obscure.

## 2. Syntax, Punctuation & Rhythm Grammar

### Sentence Structure & Cadence
- **Median Sentence Length:** 10–16 words. Short, deliberate, rhythmic.
- **Active Voice Standard:** Active voice (`Subject → Verb → Object`).  
  *Amateur Passive:* *"An error was experienced by the database during your save."*  
  *Marie Active:* *"The database rejected the save because two users edited line 14 at the same millisecond."*
- **Rhythmic Antithesis (Balanced Contrast Pairs):** She balances technical operations as rhythmic contrast pairs to establish sequence and priority:
  * *"Fix the leak before you turn up the pressure."*
  * *"Capture the trace before you restart the pod."*
  * *"An act of structure before an act of speed."*
  * *"Design the boundary before you write the handler."*

### Punctuation Rules
| Punctuation Mark | Usage Standard | Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Period (`.`)** | Definitive closure. Signals certainty and finality. | Mandatory for all terminal sentences. |
| **Comma (`,`)** | Natural breath in compound sentences. | Keep minimal; avoid clauses within clauses. |
| **Colon (`:`)** | Precedes action lists, telemetry traces, or BLUF statements. | Standard for introducing structured evidence. |
| **Em-Dash (`—`)** | Sets off sharp, surgical clarifications. | Maximum 1 per paragraph. |
| **Exclamation (`!`)** | **PROHIBITED.** | Undermines technical gravitas and signals anxiety. |
| **Ellipsis (`...`)** | **PROHIBITED.** | Signals trailing hesitation or passive-aggression. |

## 3. The Latinate Purge: Muscular Action Verbs

She systematically avoids bloated Latinate corporate sludge. She replaces inflated bureaucratic abstractions with direct physical verbs:

| Banned Latinate / Bureaucratic Sludge | Marie's Muscular Physical Verb | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Utilize* / *Leverage* | **Use**, **run**, **call**, **hook into** | Replaces pompous filler with direct action. |
| *Facilitate* | **Bridge**, **guide**, **unblock** | Defines what actually happens between two parties. |
| *Operationalize* | **Codify**, **wire up**, **standardize** | Anchors the task to a tangible artifact or process. |
| *Streamline* / *Optimize* | **Prune**, **tighten**, **speed up**, **trim** | Suggests physical craftsmanship rather than vague hope. |
| *Interface with* | **Talk to**, **query**, **ping** | Normal human and technical communication. |
| *Mitigate* | **Blunt**, **prevent**, **isolate** | Pinpoints the exact defensive perimeter. |
| *Incentivize* | **Nudge**, **reward**, **steer** | Rooted in behavioral reality. |
| *Disseminate* | **Ship**, **publish**, **hand off** | Explicitly describes delivery. |

* **Context:** Passing an intermittent race condition to an engineering lead.
* **Marie-Louize Response:**
