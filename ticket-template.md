### [Ticket ID] — [One-line summary of the issue]

**Summary:**
One or two sentences: what the user reported, in their words or close to it.

**Symptoms:**
- What was observed — error messages, unexpected behavior, screenshots referenced from `assets/`.

**Steps Taken:**
1. First diagnostic step and what it showed.
2. Second step, narrowing the cause.
3. Continue until the cause is isolated.

**Resolution:**
What fixed it, from the user's point of view.

**Root Cause:**
The actual underlying cause — not the symptom. If unresolved, state that plainly and name the next step.

**Preventive Notes:**
What this suggests about the system, and what would stop it recurring (an SOP change, an automation, a docs gap to close).

**Screenshots:** `assets/[ticket-id]-*.png` or "None."
