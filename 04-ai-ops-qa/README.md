## Operational Focus

1. **Systemic Evaluation over Vague Prompting:** Rather than endlessly tweaking system prompts to avoid hallucinations or repetitive formats, we enforce explicit algorithmic gates (entropy scoring, state tracking ledgers, source citation hashes).
2. **Deterministic Quality Gates:** Evaluating AI outputs across multi-dimensional rubrics (Factual Grounding, Pedagogical Sequencing, Diversity, Phrase Repetition) with automated pass/fail thresholds.
3. **Empirical Model Routing:** Benchmarking competing models (Gemini Flash vs. DeepSeek Reasoner) on real curriculum workloads to optimize the trade-off curve between token cost, reasoning latency, and schema fidelity.
